Run a team of agents, not a couple of chats.

A self-hosted control panel for dozens of Claude Code and Codex sessions: who leads whom, how agents reach you and each other, and the team's routine — from your phone and from your desk.

Install Source on GitHub
aacpanel

Whoever waits for you stands on top. Helpers stand under the session that opened them.

Two sessions? You don't need this.

With a couple of sessions the vendor's own client is enough. aacpanel is for when there are dozens of them, many projects and several accounts — and the agents keep working while you are away.

Built and used daily on ~40 sessions across three accounts.

Hierarchy

A lead, its helpers, and their helpers.

aacpanel

A Claude lead opens Codex helpers. Their reports come back to it, and it answers them, gives the next step and closes them when they are done.

Agent → you

Every way an agent needs you — not just a chat box.

aacpanel

A question with options, a permission, a brief for later, a task for your hands, a key it needs: each comes as its own card, and the queue over the composer says what the session waits on now.

Agent → agent

Sessions write to each other — across projects and accounts.

aacpanel

A letter can wait for an answer or for the work to be done, by a deadline. Claude writes to Codex and back, from a work account to a personal one, and both feeds keep the thread.

Long work

Work that outlives a context window.

aacpanel

Near its limit a session saves its tasks and a handoff to the panel, restarts clean and goes on from the same step. It can sleep until an alarm, and a closed conversation resumes from the archive.

Accounts

Several accounts, dozens of projects, one map.

aacpanel

Model, permission mode, context cap and the message after a restart are set once — on a project or on a whole account. Every account's limits stay in view.

Review

Review what twenty agents did — without a scrollback.

aacpanel

The conversation reads like a book. The diff and who wrote each line sit beside it, a tap away from the session that wrote it — and the project's tasks and handoffs are kept in the panel too.

Who gets in

Your machine. Your accounts. No cloud in between.

  • A passkey signs you in. No passwords to leak.
  • The panel runs on your machine, reached by your domain, your home network or your tailnet.
  • Agents work on your own accounts. The panel's container never reads their transcripts.

Install

One command. Alpha — expect rough edges.

$ git clone https://github.com/BeMySlaveDarlin/aacpanel ~/aacpanel && ~/aacpanel/install.sh

It shows its plan before it changes anything, and install.sh uninstall takes back exactly what it did.

Linux with systemd · Docker with Compose v2 · tmux and python3 · Claude Code, signed in · Codex, if you want it
