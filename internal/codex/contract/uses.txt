# What the panel reads and sends of the app-server protocol of codex.
#
# A message line names a method and what the panel does with it:
#   call    a request the panel sends; send lines are its params, read lines its result
#   answer  a request of the daemon the panel answers; read lines are its params, send lines its result
#   hear    a notification the panel reads; read lines are its params
#   tell    a notification the panel sends
#   quiet   notifications the panel turns off at the handshake
# The indented lines under a message are its places. A path goes by properties
# with dots; [] steps into the items of a list, {} into the values of a map,
# <word> into the variant of a union that word names. The words after a colon
# are the values the panel compares, maps or sends there.
